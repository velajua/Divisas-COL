Privacy policy

Privacy and use of data

Divisas COL is an informational website. You can browse the site without creating an account.

If you choose to subscribe to the newsletter, we store the email address you submit, the page or city where you subscribed, and the submission date so we can send updates and manage the list.

As with many websites, the site may record basic technical information needed to operate correctly: browser, device type, pages visited, loading errors, or similar performance data. That information is used to keep the site stable and understand which sections need improvement.

If Divisas COL shows advertising through Google AdSense or other providers, those third parties may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and, when applicable, personalize advertising based on previous visits to this or other sites.

Google may use cookies such as the DoubleClick cookie to show relevant ads. You can manage or disable ad personalization from Google's ad settings and review your browser's cookie controls.

Some links lead to external exchange-house pages, forms, or other sources. Those sites have their own privacy rules and are not controlled by Divisas COL.
